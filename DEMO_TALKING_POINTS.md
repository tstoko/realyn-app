## Objection Handling

### "We don't have many disputes"
- **Response**: "Even 10-20 disputes/month saves 20-40 hours. That's half a week of work. Plus, each dispute you win is revenue protected."

### "Our current process works fine"
- **Response**: "That's great! Realyn makes it faster and more effective. You'll win more disputes with less effort. It's an upgrade, not a replacement."

### "The AI might make mistakes"
- **Response**: "You have full control. The AI generates drafts, but you review and edit everything before submission. It's AI-assisted, not AI-automated."

### "We use a different PMS"
- **Response**: "We currently support Mews, with more integrations coming. You can also manually link disputes to bookings. The AI still helps with evidence planning and arguments."

### "It's too expensive"
- **Response**: "Let's calculate your ROI. If you have 30 disputes/month and Realyn helps you win 5 more, that's $750-1,000/month protected. The time savings alone often justify the cost."

### "We need to see it work first"
- **Response**: "Absolutely. We offer a 30-day trial. Set it up, process a few disputes, and see the results. No commitment until you're convinced."
