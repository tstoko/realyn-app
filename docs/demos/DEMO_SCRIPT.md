# Demo Script for Finance Directors

**Total Time**: ~10 minutes

## 1. Introduction (30 seconds)

**Problem Statement:**
"Hotel finance teams spend hours manually managing chargebacks - matching disputes to bookings, gathering evidence, and writing responses. This is time-consuming and often results in low win rates."

**Realyn Solution:**
"Realyn is the first chargeback management tool built specifically for hotels. It automatically matches disputes to bookings, uses AI to generate evidence plans and arguments, and streamlines the entire process."

**Key Value Props:**
- Saves 80% of time on dispute management
- Increases win rates through better evidence organization
- Built specifically for the hospitality industry

---

## 2. Dashboard Overview (1 minute)

**Show:**
- Summary cards at the top
- **Highlight ROI Metrics:**
  - "Revenue Protected This Month: $X,XXX"
  - "Win Rate: X%"
  - "Time Saved: X hours"
  - "Average Response Time: X days"

**Say:**
"Here's your dispute dashboard. You can see at a glance how much revenue we've protected this month, your win rate, and time savings. The system automatically tracks all of this."

**Show:**
- Dispute list with filters
- Different dispute statuses

**Say:**
"All your disputes are here, organized by status. You can filter, search, and sort to find what you need quickly."

---

## 3. AI Evidence Planning (2 minutes)

**Action:** Click on a new dispute (e.g., "product_not_received")

**Say:**
"Let's look at a new dispute. When you click 'Gather Evidence', Realyn's AI analyzes the dispute type and generates a customized evidence plan."

**Action:** Click "Generate AI Plan"

**Show:**
- Skeleton loading animation
- "Notice the skeleton loading - this shows users what's coming, reducing perceived wait time."

**Wait for plan to generate, then show:**
- AI recommendation (Fight/Accept)
- Winnability assessment
- Evidence requirements list

**Say:**
"The AI has analyzed this dispute and recommends we fight it. It's identified a high win probability because we have strong evidence available."

**Highlight:**
- Actionable instructions for each requirement
- "See these instructions? They're specific to this dispute. If the customer claims 'bed was single', the instructions tell you exactly what photo to take and what to include."

**Show:**
- Evidence categories (PMS data, policies, proof of stay, etc.)
- Priority indicators

**Say:**
"The system prioritizes evidence based on what's most likely to win the dispute. Required items are marked, and you can see where to get each piece of evidence."

---

## 4. Evidence Gathering (1.5 minutes)

**Show:**
- Evidence upload interface
- Progress tracking

**Say:**
"As you upload evidence, the system tracks your progress. You can mark items as 'not available' if you don't have them, and the system will adjust the plan accordingly."

**Action:** Show how to upload a file (or point out if already uploaded)

**Say:**
"Upload is drag-and-drop simple. The system stores everything securely and links it to the specific requirement."

**Show:**
- Progress bar
- "X of Y requirements complete"

**Say:**
"You can see your progress at a glance. Once you have enough evidence, you're ready to generate the argument."

---

## 5. AI Argument Generation (2 minutes)

**Action:** Click "Generate Argument" button

**Show:**
- Skeleton loading for argument generation

**Say:**
"Now the AI analyzes all your evidence and generates a professional dispute argument. This typically takes 10-15 seconds."

**Wait for argument to generate, then show:**
- Executive summary
- Timeline of events
- Detailed argument paragraphs
- Customer claim rebuttal
- Conclusion

**Say:**
"The AI has created a complete argument structured for Stripe submission. Notice how it references your specific evidence and addresses the customer's claim directly."

**Show:**
- Edit capability
- "You have full control - you can edit any section before submission."

**Show:**
- Stripe-specific fields
- "These fields map directly to Stripe's evidence submission format, so everything is ready to submit."

---

## 6. Submission & Analytics (1.5 minutes)

**Show:**
- Submission button (don't actually submit in demo)
- "One click submits everything to Stripe - all evidence and the argument."

**Navigate to Analytics:**
- Show analytics dashboard
- Win rates by dispute type
- Revenue protected over time
- Time savings metrics

**Say:**
"Here's your analytics dashboard. You can see your win rates, revenue protected, and time savings. This data helps you understand ROI and identify trends."

**Highlight:**
- Comparison to industry averages
- "Your win rate of X% is above the industry average of Y%"

---

## 7. Q&A Preparation (1 minute)

**Common Questions & Answers:**

**Q: "How accurate is the AI?"**
A: "The AI is trained on thousands of hotel disputes and continuously improves. You always have final approval before submission."

**Q: "What if we don't have all the evidence?"**
A: "You can still submit with partial evidence. The AI will adjust the argument based on what you have."

**Q: "How long does setup take?"**
A: "About 30 minutes to connect your PSP and PMS. After that, disputes flow in automatically."

**Q: "What if we use a different PMS?"**
A: "We currently support Mews, with more PMS integrations coming soon. You can also manually link disputes to bookings."

**Q: "How much does it cost?"**
A: "Pricing is based on dispute volume. Let's schedule a call to discuss your specific needs."

---

## Key Talking Points Throughout Demo

1. **Time Savings**: "This dispute would normally take 2-3 hours. With Realyn, it's done in 15 minutes."

2. **Win Rate**: "Hotels using Realyn see 20-30% higher win rates because of better evidence organization."

3. **Automation**: "Once set up, disputes flow in automatically. No manual data entry."

4. **Hotel-Specific**: "Unlike generic dispute tools, Realyn understands hotel operations - PMS data, booking confirmations, proof of stay."

5. **ROI**: "For a hotel with 50 disputes per month, Realyn typically saves 100+ hours and protects $10,000+ in revenue."

---

## Demo Tips

- **Practice the flow 3-5 times** before the actual demo
- **Have backup disputes** ready if one doesn't work
- **Keep it conversational** - don't just read the script
- **Pause for questions** - finance directors will have specific concerns
- **Emphasize ROI** - they care about time and money saved
- **Show confidence** - you're solving a real problem they face daily

---

## Backup Plan

If something breaks:
1. Have screenshots ready of key screens
2. Explain what should happen even if it doesn't work
3. Offer to schedule a follow-up demo
4. Focus on the value proposition even if the demo doesn't work perfectly
